# Privacy Policy

        Last Updated: January 2026

### 1. Introduction

          At Vela Vida, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting
          the personal data we hold about you. This policy explains how we
          collect, use, and share your information when you book a sailing
          expedition with us.

### 2. Information We Collect

          We may collect the following types of information when you inquire or
          book:

- Identity Data: Name, passport details (for
            marina/charter registration).
- Contact Data: Email address, phone number.
- Health/Safety Data: Dietary requirements, relevant
            medical conditions for safety at sea.

### 3. How We Use Your Data

        We strictly use your data to:

- Process your booking and payments.
- Register you with local port authorities and charter companies
            (legal requirement).
- Ensure your safety onboard.
- Send you trip details and logistics.

### 4. Data Sharing

          We do not sell your data. We only share necessary details with:

          - The Charter Company (for crew lists).

          - Port Authorities (as required by maritime law).

### 5. Contact Us

          For any privacy concerns or to request data deletion, please contact
          us at:
          join@velavidasails.com](index.html)
